# Backtest assumptions

A forecast for *t* is assumed available after the close of *t-1*. A long/flat decision fills at *t*'s opening price and earns the open-to-close return. Turnover is the absolute position change and costs are deducted at 0, 5, 10, and 20 bps. This illustrative backtest omits spreads, impact, taxes, financing, partial fills and corporate-action reconciliation; it is not evidence of tradability or profitability.
